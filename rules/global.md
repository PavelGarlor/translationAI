# Global rules
Apply to every translation. Lowest precedence among rule files (doc-type > pair > country > global).
Keep entries generic — no names, numbers or other personal data from real documents.

- Mirror-image (show-through) seals from another page are not described.
- When the same signature, stamp or embossed seal repeats on every page, describe it once and give the count in brackets, e.g. "/Штамп/ (3): …".
- When the translation describes a copy of another document (birth certificate, passport, etc.), include that document's number and the name it was issued to.
- Keep terminology identical across all documents of one order.
